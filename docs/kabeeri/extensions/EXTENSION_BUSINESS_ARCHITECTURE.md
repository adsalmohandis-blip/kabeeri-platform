# Extension Business Architecture

## Audiences

- Platform owner: controls governance, listing approval, monetization, and customer safety.
- Developers: build themes/plugins/packages and publish through the marketplace.
- Customers: install trusted extensions based on plan, compatibility, reviews, and support level.
- Marketers/agencies: use approved extensions to deliver customer outcomes and may resell services around them.

## Marketplace Model

Extensions can be:

- Free official assets for the Freemium entry path.
- Pro official assets unlocked by plan entitlements.
- Developer marketplace assets with review, certification, revenue share, and support obligations.
- Enterprise/private assets distributed to a single organization or tenant group.

## Revenue Paths

- Subscription upgrades triggered by entitlement limits.
- Paid themes and plugins.
- Developer marketplace revenue share.
- Agency implementation and support packages.
- Enterprise certification, private distribution, and SLA-backed support.

## Business Guardrails

- The free plan must remain usable, not a fake trial.
- Premium value should unlock at natural growth moments: custom domains, branding removal, pro themes/plugins, larger apps, AI usage, commerce scale, and team features.
- Marketplace trust is a platform asset; low-quality or unsafe extensions must not be published.
